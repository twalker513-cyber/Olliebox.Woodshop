Request a Custom Piece

Custom work is handled one piece at a time. The more detail you provide, the easier it is for us to understand the style, size, and purpose of the piece you have in mind.

Common custom requests include cutting boards, charcuterie boards, memory boxes, cigar trays, rolling trays, gifts, and one-of-one keepsake pieces.

How it works

The Custom Order Process

1. Send the request. Share the type of piece, size, style, budget, and timeline.

2. We review the details. We will follow up to confirm what is possible, discuss wood options, and answer questions.

3. We build with intention. Once approved, your piece is made with the same heart, integrity, and no-shortcut standard as every Ollie Box piece.
