Built to be used

Board Care

Every Ollie Box Woodshop board is handcrafted from real wood and designed to age beautifully with proper care.

Wood is a living material. Over time, your board will develop character, grain depth, and a patina unique to your home and use.

Hand Wash Only

Use warm water, mild soap, and dry immediately after cleaning.

No Dishwasher

Heat and moisture exposure can warp or crack natural wood.

Oil Regularly

Apply food-safe mineral oil whenever the board begins looking dry.

Long-term maintenance

Keeping Your Board Beautiful

With regular oiling and proper cleaning, your board can last for years while continuing to develop richness and character.

Avoid prolonged soaking, standing water, and extreme heat. Store your board dry and upright whenever possible.

Small knife marks and wear patterns are normal and part of the story your board develops over time.

Frequently asked

FAQ

What oil should I use?
Food-safe mineral oil or a board conditioner designed for butcher blocks.

How often should I oil my board?
Whenever the wood begins to appear dry or faded.

Can I cut directly on the board?
Absolutely. Every board is made to be used and enjoyed.
